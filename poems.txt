Title: Evening Tide
The sea forgets the shore each night,
then gently learns it back by morning light.
Soft waves, like whispered memory,
carry the moon home to the sea.

---

Title: Small Fire
Even in the dark and cold,
a little ember learns to glow.
Hold on, my heart, be brave and bright,
the dawn is walking toward the night.

---

Title: The Last Train West
Music: western, guitar, sad, acoustical

Scene: a lone cowboy on horseback in a vast red desert at dusk, long shadows, dust drifting in the wind, silhouette, cinematic western
The desert holds its silence like a grave,
red dust and one long shadow on the plain.
I rode out here to find the man I'd been,
and found the wind still calling out your name.

Scene: an old steam locomotive crossing a wooden trestle bridge over a canyon at sunset, smoke trailing, cinematic western
The midnight train still whistles through the canyon,
a lonesome sound that no one else can hear.
You said you'd come back on the westbound line,
but every coach rolls in with empty chairs.

Scene: cowboys sitting around a campfire at night under a huge starry sky, embers rising, silhouettes, cinematic western
The boys play low guitars around the fire,
and no one asks me why I sing so slow.
The embers climb to meet the cold, bright stars,
like all the words I never let you know.

Scene: a lone cowboy riding away across endless sand dunes into a grey dawn, footprints fading behind, vast and empty, cinematic western
So I ride on where the horizon bleeds,
past bones of fences no one will repair.
The tracks run on to somewhere I can't follow.
The sun comes up, and still you are not there.
